**Description**
```
The rift is eating the world one zone at a time. You go in anyway.

RIFTFALL is a top-down action RPG built around one question: do you push deeper
now, or bank what you're carrying?

LOOT THAT MATTERS
Six rarities from Common to Relic, randomised affixes, and a bag that compares
every drop against what you're wearing - DPS and survivability side by side, so
you never have to guess which sword is better.

THE RIFT CHARGE
Seal a zone and you carry a charge into the next: up to +40% damage and +50%
item luck. It survives only if you go DEEPER. Step back toward safety and it
fades.

KEYSTONES GATE THE DESCENT
The way down is locked behind keystone drops - common from zone alphas, rare
from elites, rarer still from chests. A pity counter means you are never stuck.

A LATTICE, NOT A TREE
61 hex nodes with no forced path. Walk any direction from the root, respec
whenever you want, rebuild around the gear you actually found.

FIVE ZONES, 110 ENEMIES
Verdant Hollow to Voidspire - each with its own palette, roster, elites and
alpha. Affixed enemies announce exactly what makes them dangerous.

No account. No install. No paywall. Click a class and you are fighting.
```

**How to play**
```
MOVE      WASD or arrow keys, or drag the left half of the screen on touch
ATTACK    Space, or the sword button
SKILL     Q, or the skill button
DASH      E, or the dash button
POTION    R, or the potion button
INTERACT  F at a chest, gate or obelisk

Kill enemies to collect loot and gold, then open the Bag tab to equip anything
that compares better than what you are wearing. Spend skill points in the Tree
tab. Find a keystone to unlock the gate to the next zone. Everything is fully
playable one-handed on a phone.
```

## Submission flow

1. `developer.playgama.com` → upload the zip through the game upload form.
2. The QA Tool opens automatically. Play through it — this is where the
   platform's own checks run, and it is the step that caught the load-time and
   ad-handling issues on CrazyGames.
3. Fill the required fields above.
4. Click **Submit Game** to send the build for moderation.
5. Moderation takes 1-5 business days per Playgama's wiki.

## Known gaps

- Preview videos are not supplied. Playgama does not require them.
- Tablet portrait leaves background bars down both sides — `#app{max-width:560px}`.
  Cosmetic, not a rejection criterion, still open.

  `storage.get()` and its interstitial pacing can only be exercised in the QA
