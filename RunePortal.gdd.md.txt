# RunePortal — Master Game Design Document

## Core Vision
Mobile-first browser ARPG. OSRS grind loop meets Diablo auto-combat.
Single HTML file. No install. Open in Safari/Chrome and play.

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. Spawn in open world (isometric 2D)
2. Walk around — enemies auto-aggro when in range
3. Auto-combat fires — no button mashing
4. Kill enemy — loot drops (gold + gear)
5. Walk over loot to collect
6. XP gains level up stats — gates stronger content
7. Better gear = survive tougher zones
8. Explore new portal zones with harder enemies + better loot
9. Repeat — always something to grind

## Movement
- Mobile: Virtual joystick (bottom-left)
- Desktop: WASD / Arrow keys
- Perspective: Isometric 2D (Diablo-style)
- World: Open, free roam — no waves, no corridors

## Combat
- Proximity-triggered auto-attack (no tap/click to attack)
- Player attacks nearest enemy in range automatically
- Enemies fight back
- Combat stops when player walks away or enemy dies
- Attack speed governed by Dexterity stat
- Damage governed by Strength stat

## Stats (4 core stats)
| Stat | Controls |
|---|---|
| Dexterity | Attack speed |
| Strength | Melee damage |
| Vigor | Max HP (50 + vigor * 5) |
| Intelligence | Magic damage (future) |

All stats gain XP on kills and level up independently (OSRS-style).
Level threshold: 100 * current level XP per level.

## Progression Systems
- XP earned per kill split across Dexterity, Strength, Vigor
- Gear drops modify base stats via equippedBonuses
- Gear rarities: Common (white), Rare (blue), Epic (purple)
- Gear slots: Weapon, Helmet, Chest, Legs, Boots
- Always something to level — OSRS dopamine loop

## Loot System
- Gold drops every kill (1-10g)
- Gear drops 30% chance per kill
- Player auto-collects by walking over drops
- Gold tracked in backpack
- Floating text shows +Xg on collection

## Backpack / Gear UI
- BAG button top-right opens full screen overlay
- Dark slate Diablo theme (gold accents #e8c97a)
- Two tabs: BACKPACK (item grid) and EQUIPMENT (5 slots)
- Gold display at top
- Item rarity shown via border color
- Equip/unequip from tooltip
- Close button top-right

## World Design
- Open world, zone-based exploration
- Each zone has themed enemies + a boss gate
- Portal zones: named areas inspired by OSRS zones
  (Lumbridge, Wilderness, etc.)
- Tougher enemies in deeper zones
- Boss must be cleared to unlock next portal

## Enemy Design
- Three rarities: Common, Rare, Epic
- Scale HP/damage/speed by rarity
- Wander randomly, change direction every 2-3 seconds
- Respawn after death (rarity-based timing)
- Boundary clamped to tile grid

## Save State
- Auto-save every 10 seconds to localStorage
- Saves: skills, hp, gold, items, baseStats, equippedBonuses
- Load on startup wrapped in try/catch (critical — bad save = black screen)
- Clear with localStorage.clear() in console if game breaks

## Idle / Offline Features (planned)
- Offline gold + XP accumulation
- Return to reward screen
